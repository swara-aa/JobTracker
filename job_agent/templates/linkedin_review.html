LinkedIn Browser Helper

Install the included Edge/Chrome extension once, then use its Collect visible jobs button on LinkedIn.

  1. Open edge://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Click Load unpacked and select C:\Users\prach\OneDrive\Documents\JobTracker\browser_extension.
  3. Keep this Flask app running, then open LinkedIn Jobs with USA, Past week, and Entry level filters.
  4. Scroll the results list to load cards, click the extensions icon, choose Job Tracker Helper, and click Collect loaded jobs.

The helper saves every visible card containing a job link. Hidden title, company, or location values receive an "Unknown" placeholder. LinkedIn search filters control which jobs are shown.
