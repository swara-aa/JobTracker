Privacy Policy

JobTracker AI collects the information you submit to provide daily job-match emails.

Information collected

  • Email address, name, plan choice, preferred roles, and preferred location.
  • Resume file text so the app can score job matches.
  • Email delivery history to avoid sending duplicate jobs.

How it is used

Your information is used to rank jobs, generate match explanations, send daily emails, manage preferences, and improve job recommendation quality.

Third-party processing

Job descriptions and resume-derived matching context may be processed by configured AI and email providers to generate and deliver recommendations.

Your choices

Every daily email includes links to update your preferences or unsubscribe. You can stop receiving emails at any time.

Contact

Use the unsubscribe or manage-preferences link in your email for account changes.
